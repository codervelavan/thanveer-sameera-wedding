HOW TO UPDATE IT LATER (fix a typo, change a detail, etc.)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Just tell me what to change and I'll edit the file and redeploy — the
same https://thanveer-sameera.netlify.app link updates automatically,
so guests never need a new link.

If you ever want to do it yourself without me:
1. Edit index.html in this folder.
2. Open a terminal in this folder and run:
       netlify deploy --prod --dir .
   (You'll need the Netlify CLI installed and logged in — ask me and
   I can talk you through it, or just have me make the edit instead.)


NOTE ON THE NETLIFY FREE PLAN
---------------------------------------------------------------------
You'll see a small "Powered by Netlify" badge in the bottom corner of
the page — that's normal on Netlify's free tier and doesn't affect
anything guests need to do. It can be removed with a paid Netlify plan
if you ever want to, but it's not necessary.
